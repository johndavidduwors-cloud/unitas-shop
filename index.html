Meet the founders

Kevin & Gracen Geagan

From their home in the mountains outside Asheville, Kevin and Gracen have built a community of hundreds of thousands around a simple idea: health is something you build together. Unitas is where they gather the products, practitioners and habits they actually trust, so you don’t have to sort through the noise alone.

Unitas is Latin for “oneness.” It stands for body and mind, his and hers, science and everyday life, all moving in the same direction.
