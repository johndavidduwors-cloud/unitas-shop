“Only with each other.”

KEVIN & GRACEN GEAGAN·6 MIN READ

A few years into their marriage, Kevin and Gracen made a quiet agreement: alcohol would only ever be something they share with each other. We sat down with them on their porch outside Asheville to talk about why.

Let’s start simple. What is the commitment?

GracenIt’s exactly what it sounds like. If we have a drink, we have it together. Not at a work dinner without the other person, not out with friends on our own. A glass of wine is something that belongs to us.

KevinIt’s not a rule we hold over each other. It’s more like a promise we both get to keep.

Where did the idea come from?

KevinHonestly, from noticing how often a drink was the thing that pulled us in different directions. A late night out, a different version of yourself coming home. Nothing dramatic, just small distances.

GracenWe wanted the opposite. If a drink was going to be part of our life, we wanted it to bring us closer, not further apart.

“If a drink was going to be part of our life, we wanted it to bring us closer.”

Has it changed how much you drink?

GracenA lot, actually. When it has to be shared, it becomes intentional. It’s a Friday dinner on the porch, a celebration, an anniversary. It stopped being a default.

KevinAnd when one of us is training for something or just not feeling it, the other doesn’t drink either. That part surprised me. It made us a team in a way I didn’t expect.

What do you say to people who think it sounds restrictive?

KevinI get it. But every good marriage has some things that are just yours. For us, this is one of them. It actually feels like freedom, because there’s nothing to negotiate.

GracenIt’s not about alcohol, really. It’s about choosing each other in small, ordinary moments, on purpose.

How does this connect to Unitas?

GracenUnitas means oneness. Everything we share, from our training to our lab work to the food on our table, is about doing it together. This is just one more expression of that.

KevinHealth isn’t only what you put in your body. It’s the agreements you make with the person next to you.

Any advice for couples who want to try something similar?

KevinStart with a conversation, not a rule. Ask what you want to protect.

GracenAnd make it yours. Ours is about drinking together. Yours might be no phones at dinner, or a walk every night. The point is the promise.

Unitas encourages responsible choices. If you choose to drink, please do so in moderation and only if you are of legal drinking age.
